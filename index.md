# GeoPin Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 11 October 2026**

## Introduction

GeoPin is an iOS app that lets you save your current location, give it a name, and return to it later.

This policy explains how GeoPin handles your information.

## Location Data

GeoPin uses your device's location services to obtain your current location when you choose to save it.

Saved locations, including their names, coordinates and timestamps, are stored locally on your device. GeoPin does not operate its own online account system or server to collect or store your saved locations.

You can manage and delete saved locations within the app.

## Location Permissions

You can control GeoPin's access to your location through your iPhone's Settings. If you deny location access, features that require your current location may not work.

## Maps and External Services

GeoPin can open saved locations in Apple Maps or Google Maps, depending on your preference.

When you choose to open a location in a mapping service, its coordinates are passed to that service. The service's own privacy policy governs how it handles that information.

## Siri and Widgets

GeoPin supports saving locations through Siri and a Lock Screen widget. These features use your device's location functionality to save a location.

Your use of Siri and other Apple services is also subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your saved locations remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Device backups and other iOS features may affect how app data is retained.

## Children's Privacy

GeoPin is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an online account system.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if GeoPin's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this webpage.
